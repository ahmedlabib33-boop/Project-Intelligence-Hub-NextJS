# project-intelligence-hub-website — Features Presentation Guide

## Slide 1 — Executive title

**project-intelligence-hub-website: Project Intelligence and Digital Control Platform**

## Slide 2 — Business problem

- Project information may be fragmented across files and communication channels.
- Management reporting may require manual consolidation.
- Evidence retrieval may be slow.
- Project controls, contracts, and reporting may not share one source of truth.
- Repetitive analysis consumes engineering and management time.

## Slide 3 — Application purpose

Present the application as a platform that centralizes project data, workflows, evidence, analysis, and management outputs.

## Slide 5 — Main features

- **Dashboard and KPI Analytics** — Provides management KPIs, status, trends, and summaries.
- **Cost Control and Earned Value** — Supports earned-value analysis, cost control, and forecasting.
- **Delay Analysis and Claims** — Supports delay-event analysis, concurrency, entitlement, and EOT assessment.
- **Project Planning and Scheduling** — Supports baseline review, schedule monitoring, and critical-path intelligence.
- **Reports and Export** — Produces downloadable reports, spreadsheets, documents, and presentations.
- **AI Assistant / Chat** — Conversational assistance, intelligent analysis, and tool-supported workflows.
- **Contract and FIDIC Intelligence** — Supports contract interpretation, notices, claims, and clause evidence.
- **Knowledge Search and RAG** — Retrieves relevant records, documents, and evidence.
- **Arabic, RTL, and Localization** — Supports localization and right-to-left interfaces.
- **Responsive and Mobile Experience** — Supports desktop, tablet, and mobile use.
- **Charts and Visualization** — Transforms project data into visual decision-support outputs.
- **Testing and Quality Assurance** — Provides automated quality and regression protection.
- **Document Intelligence** — Reads, extracts, analyzes, and retrieves evidence from documents.
- **Database and Persistent Storage** — Stores project records, users, workflows, and operational data.
- **Role-Based Access Control** — Restricts features and data according to user role.

## Slide 6 — User journey

1. User enters or selects project information.
2. The application validates the input.
3. The application processes data or documents.
4. The user receives analysis, evidence, dashboards, or recommendations.
5. The application generates a report or export.
6. The user validates and shares the final output.
